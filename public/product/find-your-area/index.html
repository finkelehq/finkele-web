In private preview. Book a demo to see it for your sites.

What it does not do yet

  • Only registered areas can be found. New areas are set up when you ask for them.
  • An area marked Terrain only has its elevation loaded, but nothing has produced depths there yet.
  • Evidence unavailable means the evidence for an area could not be read, not that it has none.
  • The list does not look up towns or postcodes itself: one it does not know is passed to the coverage map's place search.

Specification

What you get

Search
Matches every word you type across an area's name, place, part, county and identifier.
County filter
Ceremonial counties, each with the number of areas it holds.
Coverage map
Every registered area on a map. Zoomed out, nearby areas group into clusters, each one a button you can reach from the keyboard.
Status per area
Simulated: solver runs behind every number, with depths between runs interpolated from the two nearest. Predicted: depths come from the model. Terrain only: elevation loaded, no depths yet. Evidence unavailable: the evidence could not be read.
Lists
50 areas at a time, with Show more.
One click
Each area opens its own page and depth map.
Areas
Over 750 registered across England.

How it works

How it works

  1. Step 1

    Search

    Type a name, or choose a county.

  2. Step 2

    See the status

    Each area shows its status, in the list and on the coverage map.

  3. Step 3

    Open the area

    One click opens the area's page and its depth map.

Data inputs

What goes into it

  • Ground

    Terrain

    Environment Agency LiDAR on a 1 m grid: the bare-earth ground the water runs over.

  • Training data

    Solver runs

    LISFLOOD-FP runs over many storms on real terrain: the physics-based training data the model learns from.

  • The model

    TERRA

    Our machine-learning model, trained on those runs. From a storm's rainfall it gives water depth in metres, with an uncertainty band.

Giving confidence to act before flood becomes disaster.
