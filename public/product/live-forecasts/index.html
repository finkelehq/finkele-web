Live forecasts for your assets

Choose your sites, and we will re-run the forecast each time a new rainfall forecast is issued, with depth in metres at every asset and the time it was issued.

Book a demo What goes into a forecast

Status and limits

Status

In development

In development: not available yet. Book a demo to tell us which sites you would want it for.

What it will not do

  • It will be as early as the rainfall forecast it is given, and will add no lead time of its own.
  • Each forecast will be for that forecast's rain: no return periods.
  • The same limits as our maps will apply: bare-earth terrain, and rain falling on the area only.

Specification

What you will get

Your assets
Depth in metres at each one, with its uncertainty band and caveats, as the API returns them.
When
A new run each time a new rainfall forecast is issued.
Issue time
Every forecast will show when its rainfall forecast was issued, and the horizon its provider states.

How it works

How it will work

  1. Step 1

    Your sites

    You will list the sites you want watched.

  2. Step 2

    A new forecast

    Each time a new rainfall forecast is issued, TERRA will run for the ground around each site.

  3. Step 3

    Depth per asset

    You will see depth in metres at every asset, with the forecast's issue time.

Data inputs

What goes into it

  • You

    Your sites

    The places you want answers for: your own identifiers, with longitude and latitude.

  • Ground

    Terrain

    Environment Agency LiDAR on a 1 m grid: the bare-earth ground the water runs over.

  • The storm

    Rainfall forecast

    Forecast rain for the coming days over each area, from Open-Meteo.

  • The model

    TERRA

    Our AI model, trained on those runs. From a storm's rainfall it gives water depth in metres, with an uncertainty band.
