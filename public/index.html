The Problem

£3.3bn

in annual flood damage across the UK, projected to rise to £4.5bn by 2050. Many asset owners still lack the information they need to see flood risk at their own sites and act early, before damage occurs.

Source: Climate Change Committee, A Well-Adapted UK; 2025 prices, 2050 under 2°C of global warming.

The Solution

In seconds

Our model maps water depth in metres across a catchment from a storm's rainfall, so owners can see which streets and buildings it reaches. It works from the rainfall forecast and your location, so it is as early as that forecast.

Measured inference time on our deployed 2-CPU service: 8.5 s for a 1 km square in Leeds.

Our technology

AI trained on physics simulations

TERRA is our machine-learning flood model. We run the LISFLOOD-FP hydraulic solver through many storms over Environment Agency LiDAR terrain, and TERRA learns from those runs. Give it a storm's rainfall and it returns water depth in metres, with an uncertainty band, in seconds.

Fast

Seconds for a 1 km square

8.5 s of measured inference on our deployed service, so each new rainfall forecast becomes a depth map while it is still current.
