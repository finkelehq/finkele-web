Rainfall scenarios

Move the rainfall from 0.2 to 6 times a storm that happened and watch the flood depth respond, street by street. Every tile on the map says whether it is a solver run or a blend between runs.

Status and limits

Status

Private preview

In private preview. Book a demo to see it for your sites.

What it does not do yet

  • For areas with a solver campaign only.
  • No return periods: a scenario is a multiple of a storm that happened, not a “1 in 100 year” event.
  • 1× is that storm's rainfall total, not the storm as observed: the runs vary its length and where its peak falls.
  • A blend between runs is not itself a solver run, and the map labels it as a blend.
  • Terrain is bare earth, and rain falls on the area only, not river or coastal flooding.

Specification

What you get

Rainfall
0.2× to 6× the rainfall total of a storm that happened.
Storm length
Storms of 3 to 48 h.
Output
Water depth in metres on the area's map, changing as you move the rainfall.
Labelled tiles
Every map tile says whether it is a solver run or a blend between runs.

How it works

How it works

  1. Step 1

    Solver runs

    Physics solver runs for the area across many rainfall totals and storm lengths.

  2. Step 2

    Your choice

    You set the rainfall, as a multiple of the storm, and the storm's length.

  3. Step 3

    The map

    Each tile shows the solver run at that setting, or a blend between the nearest runs, and says which.

Data inputs

What goes into it

  • Ground

    Terrain

    Environment Agency LiDAR on a 1 m grid: the bare-earth ground the water runs over.

  • The storm

    Design storms

    A multiple of a storm that happened, with its length and where its peak falls.

  • Training data

    Solver runs

    Physics solver runs over many storms on real terrain: the physics-based training data the model learns from.
