Privacy notice

How we use the details you send us when you email us, and what you can ask us to do with them.

Last updated: 9 November 2026.

Who we are

Finkele Ltd is a private limited company registered in England and Wales, company number 16223616. Registered office: 9 Caxton House, Broad Street, Cambourne, Cambridge CB23 6JN, United Kingdom.

Finkele Ltd is the controller of the personal data described here. This means we decide why and how it is used, and we are responsible for it under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

What this notice covers

This notice covers the emails you send us, for example to ask for a demo or to make an enquiry. No form on this website collects personal data. Demo requests are made by email to the address shown on the site.

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

The website is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages). GitHub records each visitor's network address for security purposes, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive or use those records.

What we collect when you email us

We collect what you put in your email: your name, your email address, your organisation if you give it, and anything you choose to tell us in the message. Your email system also adds technical details to each message, such as the date and the sending server.

Please do not put sensitive personal information in your email. We only need to know who you are and what you are interested in.

This website is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep technical request logs under its own terms; we do not receive or use them.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • Replying to a demo request or enquiry. We use your details to reply, arrange a demo and follow up on that request. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: we have a genuine interest in answering people who contact us about our services, and you would expect us to use your details for that. You can object at any time (see Your rights).

We do not currently send news or marketing emails. If a sign-up for news returns, we will send it only with your consent and will update this notice first.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

Who handles it for us

Our mailbox is hosted by Microsoft (Microsoft 365), which acts as our processor under its data protection terms and may use the data only on our instructions.

We share your details with nobody else unless the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

  • Demo requests and enquiries: 24 months after our last contact with you, then deleted from our mailbox.

If a news sign-up returns, we will keep an address until the person unsubscribes or withdraws consent. We will then keep only the address, on a suppression list, so that we do not email them again.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct it, or to delete it;
  • ask us to restrict how we use it;
  • object to our use of it on the basis of legitimate interests;
  • ask for data you gave us in a portable format, where the law provides for it.

To use any of these rights, email or write to us using the details below. We reply within one month, as the law requires.

Contact and complaints

For any question about this notice or your data, email june.soh@finkele.com, or write to Finkele Ltd at the registered office above.

If you are unhappy with how we have used your data, please tell us first so that we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK regulator for data protection: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page if how we use personal data changes, and change the date at the top.
