API for your sites

Send coordinates and get back a depth in metres for each location, with its uncertainty band and caveats.

Status and limits

Status

Private preview

In private preview. Book a demo to see it for your sites.

What it does not do yet

  • In private preview: field names may change before a stable release.
  • Rate limits and pricing are not published yet.
  • Ground the model was not calibrated on is marked transferred or uncalibrated, and the band is only as good as that word.
  • Terrain is bare earth, rain falls on the area only, and there are no return periods.

Specification

What you get

Request
POST /assess, JSON over HTTPS, with your API key as a bearer token.
Where
Exactly one of: site_id (an area we serve), locations (your assets, each with your own id, lon and lat) or point (screen the ground around one place).
Rainfall
forecast (the coming days' rain forecast) or scenario (a multiple of a reference storm, its length and where its peak falls).
Per location
depth_m in metres, spread_m, interval_half_width_m, inside, and caveats with stable codes. A missing depth is null, never 0.
Evidence
verdict: calibrated, transferred or uncalibrated, with the nearest calibrated area.
Provenance
The model version, and digests of the terrain, rainfall and calibration used.
Window
window_m, the side of the terrain modelled around your locations. The server sets a default: too small a window leaves out ground that drains to you, and under-predicts.
Recorded jobs
POST /assessments keeps each request as a job you can fetch again.

How it works

How it works

  1. Step 1

    Your request

    Your locations and a rainfall: the forecast, or a scenario.

  2. Step 2

    Terrain and rain

    We take Environment Agency 1 m LiDAR around your locations, and the rainfall you asked for.

  3. Step 3

    The answer

    TERRA's depth at each location, with its band, caveats, evidence and provenance.

Data inputs

What goes into it

  • You

    Your sites

    The places you want answers for: your own identifiers, with longitude and latitude.

  • Ground

    Terrain

    Environment Agency LiDAR on a 1 m grid: the bare-earth ground the water runs over.

  • The storm

    Rainfall forecast

    Forecast rain for the coming days over each area, from Open-Meteo.

  • The storm

    Design storms

    A multiple of a storm that happened, with its length and where its peak falls.

  • The model

    TERRA

    Our machine-learning model, trained on those runs. From a storm's rainfall it gives water depth in metres, with an uncertainty band.
