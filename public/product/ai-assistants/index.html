For AI assistants

Ask Claude or another AI assistant about flood depth at your sites. Our MCP server will give it the same depths, bands and caveats the API returns, in plain words first.

Status and limits

Status

In development

In development: not available yet. Book a demo to tell us which sites you would want it for.

What it will not do

  • The assistant's wording will be its own, not ours: check the depths and caveats it quotes against the figures it was given.
  • It will carry the API's limits: bare-earth terrain, rain falling on the area only, and no return periods.

Specification

What you will get

MCP server
It will use the Model Context Protocol, the open standard AI assistants use to call tools.
Same data as the API
It will pass on the API's answers unchanged, so nothing is lost between our model and your assistant.
Plain words first
An answer the assistant can quote, followed by the figures.
Caveats
Every depth will arrive with its band and caveats.

How it works

How it will work

  1. Step 1

    Connect

    You will add our MCP server to your AI assistant.

  2. Step 2

    Ask

    Your question about a site will become a call to our API.

  3. Step 3

    Answer

    Your assistant will receive the API's answer unchanged, caveats included.

Data inputs

What goes into it

  • You

    Your sites

    The places you want answers for: your own identifiers, with longitude and latitude.

  • Ground

    Terrain

    Environment Agency LiDAR on a 1 m grid: the bare-earth ground the water runs over.

  • The storm

    Rainfall forecast

    Forecast rain for the coming days over each area, from Open-Meteo.

  • The model

    TERRA

    Our machine-learning model, trained on those runs. From a storm's rainfall it gives water depth in metres, with an uncertainty band.

Giving confidence to act before flood becomes disaster.
